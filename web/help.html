Getting started
WELCOME TO EDAMBILIGHT

EDAmbilight reads the colors at the edges of your screen and sends them to your RGB devices through OpenRGB, so your lights follow what is on screen.

Follow these four steps once. You can come back to this page any time from the Help button in the sidebar.

1
Make sure OpenRGB is running

EDAmbilight talks to your lights through OpenRGB. It starts it for you if it can find it.

  • OpenRGB needs administrator rights to reach your devices: when it starts, Windows shows a permission prompt. Click Yes, otherwise no device will be found.
  • In OpenRGB, open the SDK Server tab and click Start Server (default port 6742).
  • If the connection fails, check host, port and the OpenRGB path in Settings.
2
Map your strip to the screen

Open the Topology page. On first run the zones of your devices are imported from OpenRGB automatically (the Reload button does it again at any time).

  • Zones named MonitorTop, MonitorRight, MonitorBottom and MonitorLeft are the ones that follow the screen edges.
  • Any other zone name is ignored by the ambilight.
  • Use Flip / direction if the colors run the wrong way along an edge, then press Save Topology.
Tip: zone names come from OpenRGB. Create segments called MonitorTop, MonitorRight… in OpenRGB (Resizable zones & segments) or rename the zones in the editor.
3
Choose devices and switch it on
  • On the Control page the Devices list shows what will receive colors. Use each device's power button to include or exclude it.
  • Turn on the Enable switch in the Status panel. The ring pulses when the ambilight is running.
4
Fine-tune the look
  • Hover over (or Tab to) the i next to any panel title to see what each parameter does and what happens when you raise or lower it.
  • Pick a preset (Balanced, Cinema, Gaming, Calm) for a quick start; Reset restores the defaults and Advanced shows or hides the fine-tuning sliders.
  • Parameters that the selected algorithm does not use are dimmed with a Not used badge.
  • Pick what the LEDs do while the display sleeps or locks in Screen standby (fade, pulse, aurora and more); the default keeps the last colors.
  • Changes apply live: no need to save or restart.
help
Good to know
  • Black screen / dark LEDs: protected (HDCP) video such as some streaming apps cannot be captured. Try another Capture Engine on the Control page.
  • Exclusive fullscreen games: choose DXGI as Capture Engine.
  • Window closed: the app keeps running in the system tray. Right-click the tray icon to reopen or quit.
  • Logs are written to log/edambilight.log next to the exe.
